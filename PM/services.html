Our Practice Areas

We represent injured Texans in cases involving transportation accidents, personal injury, and insurance bad faith — with no fees unless we win.

Practice Area 01

Transportation Accidents

Vehicle accidents can cause life-altering injuries in seconds. Our attorneys have handled thousands of transportation accident claims and know exactly how to go up against insurance carriers and large corporations to get you what you deserve.

Car Accidents

Whether a fender-bender or a high-speed collision, we pursue full compensation for your medical expenses, lost wages, vehicle damage, and pain and suffering.

🚛

Truck Accidents

Commercial truck accidents carry enormous stakes. We have specific experience with FedEx, UPS, and other carrier claims — understanding the federal regulations and complex liability that make these cases unique.

🏍️

Motorcycle Accidents

Motorcyclists are disproportionately vulnerable on Texas roads. We fight back against insurers who try to shift blame onto riders, ensuring you receive fair compensation for your injuries.

✈️

Aviation Accidents

Aviation accident cases involve strict federal regulations and complex liability chains. Our attorneys have the experience to navigate these intricate claims on your behalf.

🚲

Bicycle Accidents

Cyclists injured by negligent drivers deserve full legal protection. We handle bicycle accident claims involving both insured and uninsured motorists throughout Harris County.

Practice Area 02

Personal Injury

Serious injuries require serious legal representation. PM Law Firm has resolved thousands of personal injury cases, recovering compensation for medical bills, lost income, rehabilitation costs, and the pain and suffering our clients endure.

🧠

Traumatic Brain Injury

TBIs can have lasting, life-changing consequences. We work with medical experts to document the full impact of your injury and fight for compensation that reflects the true long-term cost of your recovery.

🕊️

Wrongful Death

Losing a loved one to someone else's negligence is devastating. We pursue wrongful death claims with compassion and determination, seeking justice for families left behind.

🔥

Burn Injury

Burn injuries often require extensive, costly treatment. We pursue maximum damages — including future medical costs and diminished quality of life — so you're not left carrying the financial burden alone.

⚠️

Slip & Fall

Property owners have a legal duty to maintain safe premises. When they fail and you're injured, we hold them accountable — whether the accident occurred in a store, parking lot, or private property.

Practice Area 03

Insurance Bad Faith

You paid your premiums. When an insurance company denies, delays, or underpays a legitimate claim, they're not just being difficult — they may be breaking the law.

🛡️

Claim Denials

If your insurer denied a valid claim without a reasonable basis, you may have a bad faith claim. We review your policy, investigate the denial, and fight to force the coverage you're owed.

⚖️

Underpaid Settlements

Insurance companies routinely offer lowball settlements. We analyze your claim's true value and push back — through negotiation or litigation — until you receive fair compensation.

📋

Coverage Disputes

Disputes over what your policy covers can be complex. Our attorneys know Texas insurance law inside and out and will advocate aggressively on your behalf to enforce your coverage rights.

What Insurance Companies Must Do Under Texas Law

Texas insurance companies are legally required to: conduct thorough and timely claim investigations, pay approved claims without unnecessary delay, and honor payment plans until full compensation is provided.

If your insurer has failed to meet these obligations, contact us — you may be entitled to additional damages beyond your original claim.
