What to Do After a Car Accident on Icy Roads in Houston

Winter weather accidents in Houston present unique challenges. Here’s how to protect yourself and your claim.

PM Law Firm

Houston is not known for its winter weather, but when ice and freezing temperatures hit, the roads become extremely dangerous. Drivers who are unaccustomed to icy conditions, combined with infrastructure that is not designed for winter weather, can make Houston roads treacherous when temperatures drop. If you have been involved in a car accident on icy roads in Houston, here is what you need to know.

Steps to Take Immediately After an Icy Road Accident

  • Stay safe first — if your vehicle is in a dangerous position, move to safety if possible.
  • Call 911 to report the accident and request medical assistance if needed.
  • Do not admit fault — even on icy roads, liability must be investigated.
  • Document everything — photograph the road conditions, ice, your vehicle damage, and the surrounding area.
  • Exchange information with other drivers.
  • Seek medical attention even if you feel fine — adrenaline can mask injuries.

Who Is Liable in an Icy Road Accident?

Many drivers assume that icy road accidents are simply “acts of nature” for which no one is liable. This is not always the case. Drivers are still expected to adjust their speed and behavior for road conditions. A driver who was speeding, following too closely, or failed to respond appropriately to icy conditions can still be found negligent. In some cases, government entities responsible for road maintenance may also bear liability.

Challenges Unique to Icy Road Accident Claims

  • Evidence of icy conditions can disappear quickly as temperatures rise.
  • Insurance companies may argue that the weather, not the driver, caused the accident.
  • Witnesses may be difficult to locate.

For these reasons, it is important to document road conditions as thoroughly as possible immediately after the accident and to contact an attorney quickly.

Texas and Comparative Fault on Icy Roads

Even if you were also driving on icy roads at the time of the accident, you may still be able to recover compensation if the other driver was more at fault. Texas’s comparative fault rules apply — your compensation is reduced by your percentage of fault, but as long as you are not more than 50% at fault, you can still recover.
