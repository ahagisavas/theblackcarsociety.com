UPS Accident Attorneys

When Brown Comes to Town and Causes an Accident

United Parcel Service operates one of the largest ground delivery networks in the world, with tens of thousands of package cars, feeder trucks, and tractor-trailers making deliveries across the Houston metro area every day. UPS drivers are under relentless time pressure to complete enormous route loads — pressure that translates directly into aggressive, hurried driving that endangers everyone on the road. When a UPS vehicle hits your car, UPS’s in-house legal team and its network of specialized defense firms mobilize immediately. PM Law Firm fights back on your behalf with the same urgency, the same resources, and the same level of determination.

UPS Driver Employment Status

Unlike FedEx Ground, which relies heavily on independent service providers, the majority of UPS package car drivers are direct employees of UPS, covered by the Teamsters union collective bargaining agreement. This distinction has significant legal implications for your claim:

Vicarious Liability

Because UPS drivers are employees rather than independent contractors, UPS is directly and automatically vicariously liable under the doctrine of respondeat superior for accidents UPS drivers cause while on duty. There is no contractor shield to pierce.

Negligent Supervision & Training

UPS can also be held directly liable for its own negligence in training, supervising, and retaining drivers with dangerous driving histories. We investigate UPS’s internal performance systems to find evidence of prior violations.

UPS Freight Drivers

UPS Freight (now operating as TForce Freight) operates large tractor-trailers with different licensing, regulatory, and insurance structures than UPS package cars. These cases require specific FMCSA expertise.

Corporate Insurance Layers

UPS maintains substantial self-insured retention layers and umbrella policies. Our attorneys understand how to navigate these complex insurance structures to access the maximum available coverage for your claim.

UPS’s Aggressive Legal Defense

UPS has a reputation for fighting claims aggressively rather than settling quickly. Their legal strategy typically includes:

Immediate Accident Response

UPS safety managers and adjusters respond to accidents involving their drivers within hours. They document the scene, interview witnesses, and download vehicle data — all before most accident victims have retained an attorney.

DIAD Data Preservation

UPS drivers carry Delivery Information Acquisition Devices (DIADs) that record location, timing, and delivery confirmation data. UPS controls this data and may argue it is proprietary — we fight to obtain it through discovery.

Surveillance of Claimants

UPS and its insurers have been known to conduct surveillance of injury claimants to find evidence contradicting the severity of reported injuries. We advise clients on how to protect themselves while still living their lives.

Medical Record Mining

Defense attorneys will obtain your complete medical history and look for any pre-existing conditions they can use to argue that your current injuries are not caused by the UPS accident. We anticipate and counter these arguments.

Delivery Pressure and Reckless Driving

UPS drivers are under extraordinary performance pressure that directly contributes to dangerous driving. Former UPS drivers and safety experts have documented the following systemic issues:

Extreme Stop Counts

UPS package car drivers regularly make 120 to 200 or more stops per day, particularly during peak seasons. This crushing volume requires constant rushing between stops with minimal margin for safe driving practices.

Orion Routing Pressure

UPS’s proprietary Orion routing algorithm optimizes delivery sequences for efficiency. Drivers who deviate from Orion routes face scrutiny, creating pressure to follow routes that may not reflect real-world safety conditions.

Time Clock Monitoring

UPS supervisors monitor driver performance in real time via telematics. Drivers know their speed, stops, and idle time are constantly tracked, creating pressure to make up time through aggressive driving.

Peak Season Surge

During the holiday season and peak shipping periods, UPS brings on seasonal drivers with minimal training. These peak drivers have higher accident rates and operate in the same traffic conditions as experienced drivers.

Evidence in UPS Accident Cases

Building a successful UPS accident claim requires rapid, thorough evidence gathering. PM Law Firm moves immediately to secure:

Telematics & GPS Records

UPS vehicles are equipped with comprehensive telematics systems recording speed, braking, acceleration, and location. This data provides a precise reconstruction of driver behavior in the moments before the crash.

DIAD Delivery Records

The driver’s handheld device records every delivery scan with timestamps, confirming whether the driver was behind schedule and under pressure at the time of the accident.

Driver’s Performance History

We subpoena UPS’s internal performance records, safety observations (SODs), prior accident reports, and disciplinary history for the specific driver involved.

Vehicle Maintenance Logs

We obtain maintenance records for the specific UPS vehicle to identify whether brake issues, tire wear, or other mechanical deficiencies contributed to the accident.

Dashcam & Traffic Footage

We immediately issue preservation demands for any dashcam footage from the UPS vehicle and obtain traffic camera footage from the City of Houston and Harris County.

Eyewitness Statements

We locate and interview independent eyewitnesses before their memories fade and before UPS adjusters have the opportunity to take their own recorded statements.

What to Do After a UPS Truck Accident

The steps you take immediately after a UPS accident have a direct impact on the outcome of your claim. Here is what to do:

Call 911

Request police and, if needed, emergency medical services. Insist that an accident report be filed even if UPS personnel arrive and suggest it is not necessary.

Photograph Everything

Capture photos of both vehicles, the UPS vehicle’s identification numbers and markings, any skid marks or debris, traffic signals, and your injuries from multiple angles.

Get Medical Care the Same Day

Visit an emergency room or urgent care clinic immediately, even if symptoms seem minor. Delayed medical treatment is one of the most common arguments used to reduce claim values.
